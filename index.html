"We buy the same fifteen hectares every harvest. When you know the farm, you stop needing to dress up the bar."

— Noa Ferreira, sourcing lead

Each origin on this site is a single farm or a cooperative of fewer than a dozen growers. We pay above the Fairtrade floor price, publish the number on request, and roast within six weeks of the harvest date printed inside every wrapper. Nothing is blended across countries — a bar labeled Ecuador is Ecuador, start to finish.
